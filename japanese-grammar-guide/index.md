{: .fs-9 }

A complete guide to Japanese grammar, from basics to advanced topics.

## Introduction

This guide is designed to help you learn Japanese grammar in an intuitive way. It effectively explains the structure of the language, which is often very different from English.

## Table of Contents

### [Basic Grammar]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/)

Build the foundation. Learn about the state of being, particles, adjectives, and basic verb conjugations.

### [Essential Grammar]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/)

Expand your ability to communicate. Covers polite forms, compound sentences, conditionals, and more.

### [Special Expressions]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/)

Master the nuances. Includes causative, passive, honorifics, and other specific expressions.

### [Advanced Topics]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/advanced-topics/)

Polish your Japanese. Formal expressions and advanced grammatical concepts.
