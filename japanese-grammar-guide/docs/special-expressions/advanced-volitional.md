# Advanced Volitional (~Tou to suru)

{: .fs-9 }

Using the volitional form to express an attempt or effort that was about to happen.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Structure

`Volitional Form` + `to` + `suru`.

## Meaning

"Try to..." or "About to..."

## Examples

_寝ようとしたとき、電話が鳴った。(Neyou to shita toki, denwa ga natta.)_\n(Just when I was about to sleep, the phone rang.)

_逃げようとしている。(Nigeyou to shite iru.)_\n(He is trying to escape.)
