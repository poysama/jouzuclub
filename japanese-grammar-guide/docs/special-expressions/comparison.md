# Comparison

{: .fs-9 }

Comparing two or more things.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Yori (Than)

Indicates the standard of comparison.
_これはあれ**より**おいしい。(Kore wa are **yori** oishii.)_\n(This is more delicious **than** that.)

## Hou (The side of)

Indicates preference or specific properties of one side.
_これの**ほう**がおいしい。(Kore no **hou** ga oishii.)_\n(This [side] is delicious.)

## Full Set Structure

`A no hou ga, B yori property.`
_リンゴのほうが、バナナより好き。(Ringo no hou ga, Banana yori suki.)_\n(I like apples more than bananas.)
