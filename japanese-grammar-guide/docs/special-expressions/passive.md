# Passive Form

{: .fs-9 }

The passive form indicates that the subject is the **recipient** of the action, often with a nuance of suffering or annoyance.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Conjugation

### Ru-verbs

Drop `ru` and add `rareru`. (Same as potential form contextually).
_Taberu_ (食べる) → _Taberareru_ (食べられる) (Be eaten)

### U-verbs

Change final /u/ to /a/ and add `reru`.
_Iku_ (行く) → _Ikareru_ (行かれる) (Have someone go [regretful])\n*Kaku* (書く) → _Kakareru_ (書かれる) (Be written)

### Irregular

- **Suru (する)** → **Sareru (される)**\n- **Kuru (来る)** → **Korareru (来られる)**

## Usage

_私は猫にパンを食べられた。(Watashi wa neko ni pan wo taberareta.)_\n(I had my bread eaten by the cat. [Suffering passive])
