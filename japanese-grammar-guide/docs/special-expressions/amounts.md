# Expressing Amounts

{: .fs-9 }

Particles that define scopes or limits.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Dake (Only/Just)

Limits the amount.
_これだけ。(Kore dake.)_ (Just this.)

## Shika (Only... negative)

Limits the amount but implies it's insufficient. Must be followed by a **negative** verb.
_これしかない。(Kore shika nai.)_\n(There is _only_ this [and nothing else].)

## Hodo (Extent)

"To the extent of..." or "Not as much as...".
_これほどおいしいものはない。(Kore hodo oishii mono wa nai.)_\n(There is nothing as delicious as this.)
