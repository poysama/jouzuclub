# Polite Form

{: .fs-9 }

While the dictionary form is used for casual speech among friends, the polite form (often called -masu form) is essential for speaking to strangers, teachers, or coworkers.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## The -masu Stem

To create the polite form, you first need the "stem" of the verb.

### Ru-verbs

Drop the `ru`.

- _Taberu_ (食べる) → _Tabe_ (食べ)

### U-verbs

Change the final /u/ vowel sound to an /i/ sound.

- _Iku_ (行く) → _Iki_ (行き)\n- _Nomu_ (飲む) → _Nomi_ (飲み)\n- _Asobu_ (遊ぶ) → _Asobi_ (遊び)

## Conjugation

| Purpose        | Rule         | Example (Taberu)    | Example (Iku)      |
| :------------- | :----------- | :------------------ | :----------------- | --- | -------- | ------------- | ---------------------- | --------------------- | --- | ---- | --------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------- | --- | ------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Present/Future | Add **masu** | Tabemasu (食べます) | Ikimasu (行きます) | \n  | Negative | Add **masen** | Tabemasen (食べません) | Ikimasen (行きません) | \n  | Past | Add **mashita** | Tabemashita (食べました) | Ikimashita (行きました) | \n  | Past Negative | Add **masen deshita** | Tabemasen deshita (食べませんでした) | Ikimasen deshita (行きませんでした) |

## Irregular Verbs

- **Suru (する)** → **Shimasu (します)**\n- **Kuru (来る)** → **Kimasu (来ます)**
