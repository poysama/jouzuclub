# Potential Form

{: .fs-9 }

The potential form expresses the ability to do something ("can do").
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Conjugation rules

### Ru-verbs

Drop `ru` and add `rareru`.
_Taberu_ (食べる) → _Taberareru_ (食べられる) (Can eat)

### U-verbs

Change the last vowel /u/ to /e/ and add `ru`.
_Iku_ (行く) → _Ikeru_ (行ける) (Can go)\n*Asobu* (遊ぶ) → _Asoberu_ (遊べる) (Can play)

### Irregular Verbs

- **Suru (する)** → **Dekiru (できる)**\n- **Kuru (来る)** → **Korareru (来られる)**

## Usage Note

In the potential form, the object particle `wo` is often replaced by `ga`.
_日本語が話せる。(Nihongo ga hanaseru.)_ (I can speak Japanese.)

## See Also

- **Ra-nuki Kotoba**: In casual speech, "ra" is often dropped from Ru-verbs. (_Tabereru_ (食べれる) instead of _Taberareru_ (食べられる))
