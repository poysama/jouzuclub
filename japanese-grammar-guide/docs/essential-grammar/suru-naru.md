# Suru and Naru

{: .fs-9 }

Two very common verbs for describing change are `Suru` (to do/make) and `Naru` (to become).
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Naru (To Become)

Used with the particle `ni` (for nouns/na-adj) or `ku` (for i-adj).

### With Nouns / Na-adjectives

_先生になる。(Sensei ni naru.)_ (Become a teacher.)\n*きれいになる。(Kirei ni naru.)* (Become pretty/clean.)

### With I-adjectives

Drop the `i` and add `ku`.
_熱くなる。(Atsuku naru.)_ (Become hot.)

## Suru (To Make / Decide)

Used similarly with `ni` or `ku` to mean making something act in that way, or deciding on something.

### "To Make"

_部屋をきれいにする。(Heya wo kirei ni suru.)_ (Make the room clean.)\n*音を大きくする。(Oto wo okiku suru.)* (Make the sound louder.)

### "To Decide on"

_これにします。(Kore ni shimasu.)_ (I will decide on this one. [Ordering at a restaurant])
