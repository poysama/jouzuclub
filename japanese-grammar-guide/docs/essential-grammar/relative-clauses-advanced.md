# Relative Clauses (Advanced)

{: .fs-9 }

Relative clauses can act as the subject or object of a larger sentence, allowing for complex nested statements.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Replacing "Ga" with "No"

In a relative clause, the subject marker `ga` can often be replaced with `no` to make it flow smoother.

_彼が作ったケーキ (Kare ga tsukutta keeki)_ (The cake he made)\n*彼の作ったケーキ (Kare no tsukutta keeki)* (Same meaning, slightly softer)

## Using the clause as a Noun

You can treat a whole phrase as a noun by adding `no` or `koto` at the end.

_日本語を勉強するのは楽しい。(Nihongo wo benkyou suru no wa tanoshii.)_\n(Studying Japanese is fun.)

_Note: Lit. "The thing of studying Japanese is fun."_

## Nested Examples

_私が昨日見た映画は、友達が勧めたものでした。(Watashi ga kinou mita eiga wa, tomodachi ga susumeta mono deshita.)_\n(The movie I saw yesterday was the thing my friend recommended.)
