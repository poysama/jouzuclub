# Casual Patterns & Slang

{: .fs-9 }

Spoken Japanese involves a lot of slurring and contractions.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Contractions

- **~Te iru** becomes **~Teru**.\n _何してる？(Nani shiteru?)_ (What are you doing?)\n- **~Te oku** becomes **~Toku**.\n _やっとく。(Yattoku.)_ (I'll do it [in advance].)\n- **~To iu** becomes **~Tte**.\n _何って言った？(Nantte itta?)_ (What did you say?)

## Sentence Enders

- **Jan**: Short for _ja nai_. Means "Isn't it?"\n _いいじゃん。(Ii jan.)_ (Isn't it good?)\n- **Kke**: Recall particle. "What was it again?"\n _あれは何だっけ？(Are wa nan da kke?)_ (What was that again?)\n- **Mon**: Cutesy/childish reason marker.\n _だって好きだもん。(Datte suki da mon.)_ (But I like it!)

## Guys Slang

- **Sugee / Suge (すげー / すげ)**: Awesome (from Sugoi).\n- **Yabee / Yabe (やベー / やべ)**: Dangerous/Bad/Crazy (from Yabai).\n- **Umee (うめー)**: Delicious (from Umai).
