# Formal Expressions

{: .fs-9 }

Grammar used in formal writing, speeches, or older texts.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## De Aru (で ある)

The literary version of `da`. It sounds authoritative and factual.
_吾輩は猫である。(Wagahai wa neko de aru.)_\n(I am a cat. [Famous novel title])

## Nite (にて)

Formal version of the particle `de` (at/by means of).
_東京にて開催する。(Toukyou nite kaisai suru.)_\n(Will be held in Tokyo.)

## Yue (故)

Formal "because" or "reason".
_貧しさ故に... (Mazushisa yue ni...)_\n(Because of poverty...)
