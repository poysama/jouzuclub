# Nihongo Banzai


A complete guide to Japanese grammar, from basics to advanced topics.

---

## Prerequisites

**Before starting this guide, you should be familiar with hiragana (ひらがな) and katakana (カタカナ).** These are the two phonetic writing systems of Japanese and are essential for reading any Japanese text. If you haven't learned them yet, we recommend taking time to study these character sets first. Hiragana is used for native Japanese words and grammatical particles, while katakana is primarily used for foreign loanwords.

---

## Introduction

This guide is designed to help you learn Japanese grammar in an intuitive way. It effectively explains the structure of the language, which is often very different from English.

**This guide is meant to be read sequentially**, starting with Basic Grammar and progressing through Essential Grammar, Special Expressions, and finally Advanced Topics. Each section builds upon concepts from previous sections, so reading them in order will give you the best understanding of Japanese grammar.
