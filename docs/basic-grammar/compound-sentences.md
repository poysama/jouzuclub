# Compound Sentences

{: .fs-9 }

There are several ways to combine sentences in Japanese. We already learned the **Te-form** for sequences. Here are other methods.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Expressing "And" with "To" (と)

The particle `to` connects nouns.
_リンゴとバナナ。(Ringo to banana.)_ (Apple and banana.)
_Note: You cannot use `to` to connect sentences or verbs._

## Expressing "But" with "Ga" (が) and "Kedo" (けど)

Attach `ga` or `kedo` to the end of a clause to mean "but". `Ga` is more formal, `kedo` is more casual.

### Examples

_行きたいけど、お金がない。(Ikitai kedo, okane ga nai.)_\n(I want to go, but I have no money.)

_済みませんが、... (Sumimasen ga, ...)_
(Excuse me, but...)

## Expressing "Or" with "Ka" (か)

_これかそれ。(Kore ka sore.)_ (This or that.)
