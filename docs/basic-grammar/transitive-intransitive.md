# Transitive & Intransitive Verbs

{: .fs-9 }

In Japanese, verbs often come in pairs: one transitive (acts on something) and one intransitive (happens on its own).
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Transitive (Tadoushi)

Requires an active agent and a direct object (marked by `wo`).

- _Keru_ (蹴る) - to kick\n- _Akeru_ (開ける) - to open something

_Example: ドアを開ける。(Doa wo akeru.)_ (I open the door.)

## Intransitive (Jidoushi)

The action happens without a direct object. The subject (marked by `ga`) undergoes the change.

- _Aku_ (開く) - to open [by itself]

_Example: ドアが開く。(Doa ga aku.)_ (The door opens.)

## Common Pairs
