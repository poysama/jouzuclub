# Sentence-ending Particles

{: .fs-9 }

Particles at the end of a sentence changes the "tone" or feel of the statement.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## The "Ka" (か) Particle

Used to turn a sentence into a question.
_行くか？(Iku ka?)_ (Are you going?)

## The "Ne" (ね) Particle

Used to seek agreement or confirmation, similar to "right?" or "isn't it?".
_暑いね。(Atsui ne.)_ (It's hot, isn't it?)

## The "Yo" (よ) Particle

Used to add emphasis or give new information. It gives a sense of "I'm telling you this".
_明日だよ。(Ashita da yo.)_ (It is tomorrow, you know.)

## The "Yone" (よね) Particle

A combination of both. It emphasizes a fact but also seeks agreement.
_それは違うよね。(Sore wa chigau yone.)_ (That is wrong, right?)
