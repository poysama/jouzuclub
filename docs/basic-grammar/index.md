# Basic Grammar


This section covers the fundamental building blocks of Japanese grammar. Before you can construct complex sentences, you need to understand these core concepts.

## Topics Covered

1. **[State of Being]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/state-of-being)**: How to say "is" or "am" (da/desu).
2. **[Introduction to Particles]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/particles)**: Defining subject, topic, and object.
3. **[Adjectives]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/adjectives)**: Describing things with i-adjectives and na-adjectives.
4. **[Verb Basics]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/verb-basics)**: The role of verbs and basic conjugations.
5. **[Negative Verbs]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/negative-verbs)**: How to say something does _not_ happen.
6. **[Past Tense]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/past-tense)**: Talking about what happened in the past.
7. **[Te-form]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/te-form)**: Connecting actions and making requests.
8. **[Relative Clauses]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/relative-clauses)**: Modifying nouns with verb phrases.
9. **[Sentence-ending Particles]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/sentence-particles)**: Adding nuance/tone to sentences.
10. **[Compound Sentences]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/compound-sentences)**: Combining multiple thoughts.
11. **[Transitive & Intransitive Verbs]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/transitive-intransitive)**: Pairs of verbs for self-action vs. acting on something.
12. **[Conjugation Reference]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/basic-grammar/conjugation-reference)**: A comprehensive summary table of all major verb and adjective conjugations.
