# Verb Basics

{: .fs-9 }

Japanese verbs always come at the end of the clause. There are two main categories of verbs: **Ru-verbs** and **U-verbs**.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Ru-verbs (Class 2)

To conjugate these, you simply drop the `ru` and add the suffix.
_Example: 食べる (Taberu) - to eat_

## U-verbs (Class 1)

These end in a u-sound (not necessarily just the character 'u'). To conjugate, you often change the vowel sound.
_Example: 行く (Iku) - to go, 書く (Kaku) - to write_

## Irregular Verbs

There are only two main irregular verbs:

1. **する (Suru)** - to do\n2. **来る (Kuru)** - to come

## Dictionary Form

The basic form found in the dictionary is often called the "dictionary form" or "plain form". It represents the non-past (present/future) affirmative tense.

### Examples

- `Taberu` (食べる) - eat / will eat\n- `Iku` (行く) - go / will go
