## Question 1

まいにちしんぶんを　_______ 　読みます。

- a) へ
- b) を
- c) に
- d) が

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: b) を**

**Explanation:** を is the direct object particle used before transitive verbs. "Shinbun wo yomu" means "to read a newspaper." The verb 読む (yomu) is transitive and requires the direct object particle を.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- へ: Direction particle; "shinbun e" would mean "towards a newspaper" (nonsensical in this context)
- に: Location/destination particle; would suggest reading AT the newspaper rather than reading IT
- が: Subject particle; would create a grammatically confusing sentence

</details>

## Question 2

田中さんは毎週月曜日に会社_______ 行きます。

- a) に
- b) を
- c) は
- d) で

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: a) に**

**Explanation:** に is the destination particle. "Kaisha ni iku" means "to go to the company." に marks where someone is going/heading towards.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- を: Direct object particle; "kaisha wo" would mean "through/across the company"
- は: Topic particle; changes meaning to emphasize the company as a topic
- で: Location/means particle; "kaisha de" means "at the company" (where the action happens), not the destination

</details>

## Question 3

私は昨日、友達_______ 映画を見ました。

- a) へ
- b) と
- c) を
- d) に

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: b) と**

**Explanation:** と is the "together with" particle used to indicate companionship. "Tomodachi to eiga wo mita" means "I watched a movie with a friend."

**Why others are incorrect:**
- へ: Direction particle; doesn't indicate companionship
- を: Direct object particle; would require "tomodachi wo" making the friend the object being watched
- に: Could mean "to a friend" or "for a friend," but doesn't express "together with"

</details>

## Question 4

このケーキはチョコレート_______ 作られました。

- a) を
- b) で
- c) から
- d) に

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: b) で**

**Explanation:** で marks the material/ingredient used to make something. "Chokoreto de tsukuraremashita" means "It was made with chocolate." In passive constructions, で shows what the action was made from.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- を: Direct object particle; doesn't work for ingredients
- から: "From" particle; used for sources and origins, but less precise for materials in this context
- に: Destination/target particle; wrong for indicating materials

</details>

## Question 5

私は毎朝、6時_______ 起きます。

- a) に
- b) で
- c) を
- d) へ

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: a) に**

**Explanation:** に marks a specific point in time. "Roku-ji ni okimasu" means "I wake up at 6 o'clock." When specifying an exact time, に is required.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- で: Used for duration or means ("for 6 hours" or "by means of"); not for specific time points
- を: Direct object particle; doesn't apply to time expressions
- へ: Direction particle; has no meaning with time expressions

</details>

## Question 6

兄は弟に本_______ あげました。

- a) へ
- b) を
- c) に
- d) が

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: b) を**

**Explanation:** を is the direct object particle. "Hon wo ageru" means "to give a book." The book (the thing being given) takes the を particle.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- へ: Direction particle; doesn't work with "give"
- に: "Ni" would follow the person receiving in this context "nimotsae to ageru" (give to someone), but the book itself needs を
- が: Subject particle; would make the book the subject rather than object

</details>

## Question 7

雨が降っている_______ 、学校に行きました。

- a) けれど
- b) のに
- c) ので
- d) たら

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: b) のに**

**Explanation:** のに expresses contrast or "despite." "Ame ga futte iru noni, gakko ni itta" means "Despite it raining, I went to school." This shows doing something contrary to the circumstances.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- けれど: "But/however"; works but is less specific about the contrast/opposition
- ので: "Because/so"; would suggest the rain caused going to school, which doesn't make sense
- たら: "If"; conditional form; doesn't fit the sentence structure

</details>

## Question 8

この問題は難しい_______ 、誰もできなかった。

- a) ので
- b) のに
- c) から
- d) けれど

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: c) から**

**Explanation:** から means "because" and shows the reason or cause. "Kono mondai wa muzukashii kara, dare mo dekinakatta" means "Because this problem is difficult, no one could do it."

**Why others are incorrect:**
- ので: Also means "because" but is more formal; both work, but から is more common in casual speech
- のに: Means "despite/in spite of"; opposite meaning here
- けれど: Means "but/however"; suggests contrast rather than causation

</details>

## Question 9

彼女に別れ_______ 告げられました。

- a) を
- b) に
- c) へ
- d) と

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: a) を**

**Explanation:** を is the direct object particle. "Wakare wo tsugeru" means "to tell of a breakup/to tell someone you're breaking up." The breakup (what is being told) takes を.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- に: Would suggest telling TO the breakup (nonsensical)
- へ: Direction particle; doesn't work with this verb
- と: "Together with" or used with quotations; doesn't fit here

</details>

## Question 10

毎日運動_______ 健康になります。

- a) で
- b) に
- c) を
- d) して

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: d) して**

**Explanation:** して is the te-form of する used to show "by doing" or "through doing." "Mainichi undo shite, kenkou ni narimasu" means "By exercising every day, I become healthy." It shows the means or method.

**Why others are incorrect:**
- で: Marks location or means, but in this context doesn't create the right flow
- に: Destination/target particle; wrong for this structure
- を: Direct object particle; "undo wo" would need a transitive verb following

</details>
