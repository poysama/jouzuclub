# Conditionals

{: .fs-9 }

Japanese has four different ways to say "If".
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## 1. To (と)

Natural consequence or inevitable result. "Whenever A happens, B happens."
_春が来ると、花が咲く。(Haru ga kuru to, hana ga saku.)_\n(When spring comes, flowers bloom.)

## 2. Ba (ば)

Hypothetical condition. Focuses on the condition itself.
_行けばわかる。(Ikeba wakaru.)_\n(If you go, you will understand.)

**Conjugation:**

- U-verbs: Change /u/ to /e/ + `ba` (_Kakeba_ (書けば))\n- Ru-verbs: Drop `ru` + `reba` (_Tabereba_ (食べれば))

## 3. Tara (たら)

Context-bound condition. "If/When A happens, then B." Very versatile.
**Conjugation:** Past Tense (Ta-form) + `ra`.
_時間があったら、行く。(Jikan ga attara, iku.)_\n(If I have time, I will go.)

## 4. Nara (なら)

Contextual condition based on what strictly assumes to be true. "If that is the case..."
_行くなら、私も行く。(Iku nara, watashi mo iku.)_\n(If [it is the case that] you are going, I will go too.)
