# The Question Marker

{: .fs-9 }

In polite speech, the question mark is often replaced by the particle `ka`.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Usage

Simply add `ka` to the end of a polite setence. No question mark is strictly necessary in traditional writing, though the modern period `.` or `?` is often used.

_行きましたか。(Ikimashita ka?)_ (Did you go?)

## Casual Questions

In casual speech, do **not** use `ka` to ask yes/no questions. It can sound surprisingly sarcastic or harsh. Instead, just raise your intonation.

_食べる？(Taberu?)_ (Will you eat? ↗)

## Question Words

- **Dare (誰)**: Who\n- **Nani (何)**: What\n- **Doko (どこ)**: Where\n- **Itsu (いつ)**: When\n- **Doushite (どうして) / Naze (なぜ)**: Why\n- **Dou (どう)**: How

_どこに行きますか？(Doko ni ikimasu ka?)_ (Where are you going?)
