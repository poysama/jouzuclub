# Casual Patterns & Slang

## Vocabulary

1. 何 【なに・なん】 – what
2. する (irregular) – to do
3. 言う 【い・う】 (u-verb) – to say
4. いい (i-adj) – good
5. 好き 【す・き】 (na-adj) – like
6. すごい (i-adj) – amazing
7. やばい (i-adj) – dangerous, crazy
8. うまい (i-adj) – delicious, skilled
9. 遅刻 【ち・こく】 – being late
10. 優しい 【やさ・しい】 (i-adj) – kind, gentle

Spoken Japanese involves a lot of slurring and contractions.

## Contractions

### ～ている → ～てる

```text
何してる？ - What are you doing?
食べてる？ - Are you eating?
見てる。 - I'm watching.
```

### ～ておく → ～とく

```text
やっとく。 - I'll do it (in advance).
買っとく。 - I'll buy it (in advance).
言っとくね。 - I'll tell them (beforehand).
```

### ～という → ～って

```text
何て言った？ - What did you say?
それって本当？ - Is that true?
彼女って優しいね。 - She's kind, isn't she?
```

## Sentence Enders

### じゃん (Short for じゃない)

Means "Isn't it?" or "Right?"

```text
いいじゃん。 - That's fine, isn't it?
かわいいじゃん。 - That's cute!
できるじゃん。 - You can do it!
```

### っけ (Recall particle)

"What was it again?"

```text
あれは何だっけ？ - What was that again?
名前は何だっけ？ - What was the name again?
```

### もん (Cutesy/childish reason marker)

```text
だって好きだもん。 - Because I like it!
知らないもん。 - I don't know! (childish)
```

## Guys' Slang

- すげー / すげ - Awesome (from すごい)
- やべー / やべ - Crazy/Bad (from やばい)
- うめー - Delicious (from うまい)

```text
すげー！ - Awesome!
やべー、遅刻した。 - Crap, I'm late.
これ、うめー！ - This is delicious!
```
