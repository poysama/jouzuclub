# Essential Grammar


This section covers the grammar necessary to survive basic Japanese communication. It moves from simple existence to polite speech, requests, and expressing potential or desire.

## Topics Covered

1. **[Polite Form]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/polite-form)**: Converting dictionary forms to polite -masu/desu forms.
2. **[Addressing People]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/addressing-people)**: Proper titles and pronouns.
3. **[The Question Marker]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/question-marker)**: Formal questions.
4. **[Compound Sentences]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/compound-sentences)**: More advanced connections.
5. **[Potential Form]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/potential-form)**: Expressing ability (can do).
6. **[Suru and Naru]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/suru-naru)**: "To do" vs "To become".
7. **[Conditionals]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/conditionals)**: If/When statements (to, ba, tara, nara).
8. **[Must / Have to]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/must-have-to)**: Expressing obligation.
9. **[Desire & Suggestions]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/desire-suggestions)**: "I want to..." and "Let's...".
10. **[Relative Clauses Advanced]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/relative-clauses-advanced)**: Nested relative clauses.
11. **[Giving and Receiving]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/giving-receiving)**: The complex world of giving verbs.
12. **[Making Requests]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/making-requests)**: Politely asking for things.
13. **[Numbers and Counting]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/numbers-counting)**: Counters for different objects.
14. **[Casual Patterns & Slang]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/essential-grammar/casual-slang)**: Common spoken colloquialisms.
