# Reading Comprehension Practice

Test your reading comprehension with short passages and questions.

## Question 1

次のテキストを読んでください：

「私は毎日、朝7時に起きます。そして、朝食を食べた後で、会社に行きます。」

質問：この人は何時に起きますか？

- a) 6時
- b) 7時
- c) 8時
- d) 9時

<details markdown="block">
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct Answer: b) 7時**

**Explanation:** The text clearly states 「私は毎日、朝7時に起きます」which means "I wake up at 7 o'clock in the morning every day."

**Why others are incorrect:**
- 6時: Not mentioned in the text
- 8時: Not mentioned in the text
- 9時: Not mentioned in the text

</details>

- 学校と公園: Not mentioned in the text
- 会社と商店: Not mentioned in the text
- 駅と銀行: Not mentioned in the text

</details>

## Question 5

- c) 春
- d) 秋

## Practice Tips

- Read the passage carefully before looking at the questions
- Pay attention to key words like time markers, locations, and actions
- Look for specific details mentioned in the text
- Don't assume information that isn't explicitly stated
- Review grammar points to better understand sentence structure
