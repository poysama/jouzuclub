# Tendencies

{: .fs-9 }

Describing how things or people tend to act.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

## Garu (Outward signs of feeling)

Turns an internal adjective (like "hoshii" - want) into an observation of someone else's behavior.
_Drop `i` add `garu`._
_Hoshii_ (欲しい) (I want) -> **Hoshigaru (欲しがる)** (He/She acts like they want)

## Gachi (Tend to...)

Negative habit or tendency.
_Drop `masu` add `gachi`._
_Wasuregachi_ (忘れがち) (Tend to forget)\n*Byoukigachi* (病気がち) (Tend to be sick)
