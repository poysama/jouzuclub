# 🎯 Special Expressions

This section covers expressions that add specific nuances to your sentences, such as forcing someone to do something, things happening to you, or expressing uncertainty.

## Topics Covered

1. 🫵 **[Causative]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/causative)**: Making/Letting someone do something.
2. 🪞 **[Passive]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/passive)**: Passive voice (was done by).
3. 😤 **[Causative-Passive]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/causative-passive)**: Being made to do something.
4. 🎩 **[Honorific and Humble Forms]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/honorific-humble)**: Keigo .
5. 😅 **[Unintentional Actions]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/unintentional)**: Doing things by accident (~te shimau).
6. 📦 **[Generic Nouns]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/generic-nouns)**: Tokoro, Mono, Koto.
7. 🤔 **[Certainty]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/certainty)**: Kamoshirenai, Nichigainai.
8. 📏 **[Expressing Amounts]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/amounts)**: Dake, Shika, Hodo.
9. 👂 **[Similarity and Hearsay]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/similarity-hearsay)**: You, Mitai, Sou.
10. ⚖️ **[Comparison]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/comparison)**: Comparing two things.
11. 🚧 **[Non-feasibility]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/non-feasibility)**: Impossible to do (~you ga nai).
12. 📈 **[Tendencies]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/tendencies)**: Garu, Gachi.
13. 🎯 **[Advanced Volitional]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/special-expressions/advanced-volitional)**: Trying to do something (~you to suru).
